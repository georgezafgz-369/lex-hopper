# Where Lex Hopper's words first came from — licences and credits

Lex Hopper's databases (`data/lexicon/`, `data/texts/`) were seeded once from
the sources below. The source files themselves were removed (everything in
them is in our CSV files), but their licences still apply to the data that
came from them, so their texts and the credits they ask for are kept here.
Show these in the app's About / credits screen before publishing.

| Source | Used for | Licence | What it asks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Princeton WordNet 3.0** | English words, meanings, examples, synonyms, opposites, crossword clues | WordNet 3.0 licence (`wordnet_LICENSE.txt`) | Keep its copyright and licence notice with copies of the data |
| **Greek WordNet** (Open Multilingual Wordnet copy of github.com/okfngr/wordnet) | Greek words and meanings, and the English–Greek meaning links | Apache 2.0 (`greek_wordnet_LICENSE.txt`) | Include the licence text |
| **CMU Pronouncing Dictionary** | English pronunciation (IPA) and syllables | BSD-style (`cmudict_LICENSE.txt`) | Keep its copyright notice |
| **Leipzig Corpora Collection** — Greek news 2023 | How common Greek words are (crossword difficulty) | CC BY 4.0 | Credit: "Leipzig Corpora Collection: Greek news corpus based on material from 2023" |
| **Wikidata lexemes** | Everyday Greek words and noun plurals | CC0 | Nothing required (nice to mention) |
| **Medical sources**: ICD-10 (WHO 2019), NCI Thesaurus, MONDO, Human Phenotype Ontology, Disease Ontology, Uberon, Medical Action Ontology, Cell Ontology, Symptom Ontology | English medical terms and their meanings | Each its own terms | Credit each; check each allows commercial redistribution before publishing |
| **The phone's translator** (Google ML Kit, on device) | Machine translations marked `machine` in the CSV files | ML Kit terms | — |
| **Lex Hopper** | Phrase, idiom and proverb lists, Greek grammar words, hand-written clue translations, and every edit since | Ours | — |
